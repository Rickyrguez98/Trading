How the last column is made. For each stock you hold, 2,000 possible next months (21 trading days) are simulated by resampling its own past daily moves, with the trend removed: the model has no view on direction, only on how far the price usually travels. The range is the average highest and lowest close of those months. The sell limit is the price reached in half of them, so a limit order there has about an even chance of filling within a month; the stop is the price reached in the weakest quarter of them. Percentages compare each level with what that lot cost you. "Range held" counts past months in which the real price ended inside the model's 80% band (it should be about 8 in 10). Closing prices only — intraday swings are wider. These are statistical levels from past volatility, not a prediction or financial advice.
