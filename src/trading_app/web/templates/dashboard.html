Markets

Daily prices for the last 12 months, adjusted for splits and dividends.

How to read these: the 50- and 200-day moving averages show trend (50 above 200 is the classic "golden cross"); Bollinger bands show where price sits against its recent volatility; RSI above 70 or below 30 marks stretched momentum; Fibonacci levels are the 23.6–78.6% retracements of the year's swing between its low and high. They describe past prices only — they are not forecasts or recommendations. The sector view rebases every stock to 0% a year ago and compares an equal-weight sector index with the S&P 500. Prices are cached for six hours.
